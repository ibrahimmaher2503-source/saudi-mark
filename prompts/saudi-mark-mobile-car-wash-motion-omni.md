# Saudi Mark — Omni Gemini Motion Graphics Prompt

## Concept

Create a premium, fully animated vertical social-media advertisement for **Saudi Mark**, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

Core message:

> The customer does not need to drive to a car wash. Saudi Mark comes to the customer’s location.

This is an original brand execution inspired only by the structural simplicity of modern Arabic service advertisements. Do not copy any existing brand, logo, color system, layout, wording, or video.

## Production format

- Vertical 9:16
- 1080 x 1920 composition
- 19–20 seconds
- Designed for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Snapchat
- Fully animated 2D motion graphics with light 2.5D depth
- Clean vector shapes, smooth easing, polished commercial finish
- No live-action footage
- No realistic people
- No generated logo
- No generated Arabic or English text
- No watermark
- Arabic typography, official Saudi Mark logo, and final CTA will be added later in CapCut

## Brand palette

Use a distinctive Saudi Mark palette:

- Deep navy: `#071A2B`
- Near black: `#050A10`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Aqua: `#31D6D0`
- Cyan highlight: `#7DEFF0`
- Metallic silver: `#C8D2D9`
- Restrained warm gold accent: `#E7B85A`

The first scenes should use a mostly white background with navy and silver line art. The solution and final scenes should transition into deep navy. Aqua/cyan represents water and movement. Gold is only a small premium accent, never the dominant color.

## Visual direction

- Minimal but not empty
- Strong Arabic editorial typography spaces
- Large clear visual hierarchy
- Thin line-art city elements
- Rounded geometric shapes
- Soft grain-free gradients
- Smooth path animation
- Water ripple transitions
- Clean route-line movement
- Controlled sparkle effects
- Premium service-advertising mood
- Fast enough for social-media retention, but calm and trustworthy

Keep generous empty areas in every scene for Arabic text to be composited later in CapCut. Do not fill the entire frame with decorative elements.

# Shot-by-shot animation plan

## Scene 1 — Busy day / time pressure

**Time:** 0.0–3.0 seconds

Start with a clean warm-white background. Introduce very light grey line-art buildings using `building-skyscraper.svg`, placed near the lower third and kept subtle.

Animate the `car.svg` from the left into the center with a short smooth ease-out. Add the `clock.svg` above or beside the car. The clock hand rotates quickly for one short cycle, then pauses. Add two or three small route dots moving horizontally to imply a busy schedule.

Use a gentle camera push-in of approximately 3–5%. Add a small navy pulse around the clock and a restrained gold accent on the clock hand.

Leave a large clear upper area for the first Arabic text.

Suggested CapCut text:

```text
كثيرة مشاويرك؟
```

Animation behavior:

- Text: soft fade plus upward rise
- Car: slide in from left
- Clock: scale from 85% to 100%
- Route dots: continuous short travel

## Scene 2 — The car needs care

**Time:** 3.0–6.0 seconds

Keep the white background but introduce the `car.svg` again in a larger central position. Overlay a few small grey dust particles and subtle diagonal marks. Use `clock.svg` and `calendar-event.svg` as small floating schedule symbols around the car.

Animate the dust particles in loose circular movement, then make them fade away as a turquoise glow begins to form around the car. Do not make the car dirty in a photorealistic way; keep it graphic and clean.

Use a quick horizontal wipe made from thin aqua lines to transition into the next scene.

Suggested CapCut text:

```text
وسيارتك دايمًا تحتاج عناية؟
```

Text must remain readable, centered, and limited to one or two lines.

## Scene 3 — Do not drive to the car wash

**Time:** 6.0–9.0 seconds

Use a dramatic color transition: the white background is covered by an aqua water ripple, which resolves into a deep navy background.

Place the `car.svg` on the left and a simplified distant service/building symbol on the right using `building-skyscraper.svg`. Draw a long curved route between them using the `route.svg` or `map-route.svg` reference. Animate the route slowly from the car toward the distant destination, then interrupt it with a clean diagonal slash made from a navy/aqua shape.

The message is that the customer should not spend time driving to a distant wash location.

Suggested CapCut text:

```text
لا تروح للمغسلة
```

Text treatment:

- White, bold Arabic type
- Large scale
- Strong contrast against deep navy
- One short line only
- Subtle scale-up on the final word

## Scene 4 — Saudi Mark comes to the customer

**Time:** 9.0–13.0 seconds

Bring in `saudi-arabia-location-map.svg` as a simplified background reference. Do not show tiny labels or readable map text. Crop or mask the map so it works as a clean graphic shape.

Zoom toward the western region and use `map-pin.svg` to identify Jeddah. Do not invent exact street boundaries or claim coverage beyond what the brand has approved.

Animate a glowing aqua route using `map-route.svg` or `route.svg`. The route should travel from a small service-origin dot to the Jeddah pin. Add a second smaller pulse at the destination. Use two or three moving dots, not a cluttered network.

Place the `car.svg` near the destination pin and move it a short distance along the route. Add a restrained cyan trail behind it.

Suggested CapCut text:

```text
Saudi Mark تجيك لموقعك
```

Keep the brand name in the official brand typography if provided. Do not ask Omni to render the brand name.

## Scene 5 — Easy booking and premium care

**Time:** 13.0–17.0 seconds

Transition from the destination pin into the `device-mobile.svg`. The phone enters from the bottom with a smooth spring-like ease, but no exaggerated bounce.

Inside the phone, show abstract UI blocks only. Use `calendar-event.svg`, `map-pin.svg`, and a simple checkmark-style motion shape. Do not generate readable interface text.

Around the phone, animate:

- `droplet.svg` moving in a short arc
- `bubble.svg` rising and scaling down
- `sparkles.svg` appearing only at key beats
- One thin aqua water wave crossing the lower third
- A small gold confirmation pulse after the booking tap

The phone should rotate no more than 4 degrees. Keep the composition clean and premium.

Suggested CapCut text:

```text
احجز من جوالك
وخلي العناية علينا
```

Use two lines maximum. The first line appears at 13.2 seconds; the second line appears at 14.5 seconds.

## Scene 6 — Premium final card

**Time:** 17.0–20.0 seconds

Fade all icons into a deep navy/near-black premium background. Reintroduce the `car.svg` as a clean central silhouette with metallic-silver line treatment. Keep its design identical to earlier scenes.

Animate a smooth aqua light sweep from left to right across the car. Add a small number of controlled `sparkles.svg` bursts and two or three floating `droplet.svg` elements. Add a subtle ground reflection using a soft navy-to-cyan gradient shape.

Create a clean empty area at the top or center for the official Saudi Mark logo. Create a separate empty area at the bottom for the Arabic CTA. Do not generate either one.

Suggested CapCut text:

```text
غسيل سيارات متنقل في جدة
احجز الآن
```

End with a 6-frame sparkle hold, then cut cleanly.

# Motion and editing rules

- Use ease-in/ease-out on all icons
- Use path-follow animation for route dots
- Use match cuts between pin, route, phone, and water drop
- Use water-ripple wipes only at major section changes
- Use short whoosh effects on scene transitions
- Use a soft click on the booking confirmation
- Use a light map-pin ping when the Jeddah pin appears
- Use a restrained sparkle sound on the final logo reveal
- Avoid shaky motion, chaotic particles, or excessive zooms
- Avoid fast unreadable text animation
- Avoid changing the car shape or color between scenes
- Avoid fake road names, city labels, phone numbers, prices, offers, or coverage claims
- Keep visual rhythm approximately 3 seconds per scene

# Negative prompt

No live action, no photography, no realistic people, no faces, no generated logo, no brand imitation, no generated Arabic text, no generated English text, no random letters, no watermark, no fake app name, no fake prices, no fake phone number, no exact unapproved service coverage, no tiny unreadable map labels, no distorted car, no extra wheels, no changing car model, no broken phone, no unreadable UI, no flicker, no morphing, no chaotic particles, no excessive bubbles, no oversaturated neon, no horror mood, no dark dirty atmosphere, no traffic accident, no photorealistic wash-station scene.

5. Add the Arabic copy manually using a clean Arabic display font.
6. Use white text on navy and navy text on white; use aqua only for emphasis.

8. Export at 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, high quality.
9. Verify Arabic text, logo proportions, safe margins, and readability before publishing.
