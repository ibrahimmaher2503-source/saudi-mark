# Omni Gemini Video Prompt 01 — Hook: Busy Day

Generate a complete 3-second vertical video clip, not a still image, for Saudi Mark, a premium mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

OUTPUT:
- 9:16 vertical social-media video
- 1080x1920
- 30 fps
- exactly 3 seconds
- polished 2D motion graphics with subtle 2.5D depth
- warm-white background
- no live action, no photography, no people

REFERENCE IMAGE:
Use `assets/png/car.png` as the exact car silhouette reference. Use `assets/png/clock.png` as the exact clock icon reference. Use `assets/png/building-skyscraper.png` for subtle city line art. Preserve their silhouettes; do not redesign them.

BRAND LOOK:
Deep navy #071A2B, white #FFFFFF, aqua #31D6D0, cyan #7DEFF0, metallic silver #C8D2D9, restrained gold #E7B85A. Use a mostly white background, navy line art, aqua motion accents, and only a small gold clock-hand accent.

START FRAME:
At 0.0 seconds, show a clean warm-white background with very subtle silver city line art along the bottom 15%. The car is off-screen. The frame must have a large empty upper half.

ANIMATION:
1. At 0.2 seconds, the car reference image slides smoothly from the left edge into the center, easing out.
2. At 0.7 seconds, the clock reference image scales from 85% to 100% above the car.
3. From 0.8 to 2.1 seconds, rotate the clock hand quickly through one controlled cycle, then stop.
4. From 1.0 to 2.5 seconds, animate three small navy route dots moving horizontally behind the car.
5. At 2.2 seconds, create one soft aqua pulse around the car.
6. From 2.6 to 3.0 seconds, hold the composition stable for text overlay in CapCut.

CAMERA:
Use a gentle 3% push-in from 0.0 to 2.5 seconds. No shake, no zoom jump, no rotation of the whole frame.

END FRAME:
The car is centered, clock above it, route dots paused, city line art still subtle, and the top half remains empty for Arabic typography.

DO NOT GENERATE:
No readable text, no Arabic letters, no English letters, no logo, no watermark, no fake brand name, no prices, no people, no realistic road, no extra wheels, no distorted car, no morphing, no flicker, no clutter, no excessive particles.
