Use `assets/png/saudi-arabia-location-map.png` as the exact Saudi Arabia map silhouette. Use `assets/png/map-pin.png` for the location pin, `assets/png/map-route.png` for the route, and `assets/png/car.png` for the car. Preserve the supplied shapes and do not invent map labels.

CAMERA:
Smooth map zoom only. No spin, no shake, no satellite imagery, no excessive route lines.

END FRAME:
