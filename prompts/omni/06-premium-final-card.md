# Omni Prompt 06 — Premium Final Brand Card

Create a premium fully animated vertical end-card clip for Saudi Mark, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

FORMAT:
- Vertical 9:16
- 3 seconds
- Deep navy and near-black premium background
- Clean 2D vector car silhouette with subtle 2.5D depth
- No live action, no people, no photography
- No readable text, no Arabic or English letters, no generated logo, no watermark
- Reserve clear empty spaces for the official Saudi Mark logo and Arabic CTA to be added in CapCut

USE AS VISUAL REFERENCES:
- supplied car.svg for the car silhouette
- supplied droplet.svg for water droplets
- supplied sparkles.svg for sparkle effects

ACTION:
Start with the supplied car icon centered on a deep navy-to-near-black background. Treat the car with metallic silver line art and a subtle cyan edge glow. Create a smooth aqua light sweep traveling from the front of the car to the rear, revealing a polished metallic highlight. Add two or three small water droplets floating near the lower corners. Add no more than three controlled sparkle bursts: one on the hood, one on the roofline, and one near the rear light.

Add a subtle soft ground reflection beneath the car using a navy-to-cyan gradient shape. Keep the top-center or center area completely clean for the official Saudi Mark logo. Keep the bottom quarter clean for Arabic CTA text. Finish with a 6-frame sparkle hold and a gentle 2% camera push-in.

PALETTE:
Deep navy #071A2B, near black #050A10, white #FFFFFF, aqua #31D6D0, cyan #7DEFF0, metallic silver #C8D2D9, restrained gold #E7B85A. Use gold only as a tiny premium glint if needed.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No generated text, no fake brand name, no invented logo, no watermark, no random letters, no extra cars, no distorted car, no changing car silhouette, no extra wheels, no excessive lens flares, no neon overload, no photorealistic background, no people, no flicker, no morphing, no clutter.
