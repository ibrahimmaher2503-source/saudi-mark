# Omni Prompt 01 — Hook: Busy Day

Create a premium fully animated vertical motion-graphics clip for Saudi Mark, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

FORMAT:
- Vertical 9:16
- 3 seconds
- Clean 2D vector motion graphics with subtle 2.5D depth
- White or warm-white background
- No live action, no realistic people, no photography
- No readable text, no Arabic or English letters, no logo, no watermark
- Leave the upper half of the frame clean for Arabic text to be added later in CapCut

USE AS VISUAL REFERENCES:
- supplied car.svg for the car silhouette
- supplied clock.svg for the time icon
- supplied building-skyscraper.svg for subtle city line art

BRAND PALETTE:
Deep navy #071A2B, near black #050A10, white #FFFFFF, aqua #31D6D0, cyan #7DEFF0, metallic silver #C8D2D9, restrained gold #E7B85A.

ACTION:
Start with a quiet warm-white background and extremely subtle silver-grey Jeddah-inspired skyline line art along the bottom edge. Animate the supplied car icon smoothly from left to center with a short ease-out. Place the clock icon above the car. The clock hand rotates quickly for one cycle, then pauses. Add three small navy route dots traveling horizontally behind the car to suggest a busy schedule. Add a restrained gold accent only on the clock hand and a soft aqua pulse around the car at the end.

CAMERA AND MOTION:
- Very gentle 3–5% camera push-in
- Smooth professional easing
- No camera shake
- No chaotic particles
- Keep the car silhouette consistent and centered
- End with a clean 4-frame hold so the CapCut text remains readable

COMPOSITION:
The car and clock occupy the middle third. Keep a large empty area above them for the Arabic hook. Do not fill the frame with decorative elements.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No generated text, no fake brand name, no logo, no distorted car, no extra wheels, no realistic street, no human faces, no random letters, no watermark, no excessive neon, no clutter, no morphing, no flicker.
