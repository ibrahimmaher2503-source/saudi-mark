# Omni Prompt 04 — Saudi Mark Comes to You

Create a premium fully animated vertical motion-graphics clip for Saudi Mark, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

FORMAT:
- Vertical 9:16
- 4 seconds
- Clean 2D vector map animation with light 2.5D depth
- Deep navy background with aqua route animation
- No live action, no realistic people, no photography
- No readable map labels, no Arabic or English text, no logo, no watermark

USE AS VISUAL REFERENCES:
- supplied saudi-arabia-location-map.svg as the map silhouette
- supplied map-pin.svg for the location marker
- supplied map-route.svg or route.svg for the route
- supplied car.svg for the customer car

ACTION:
Reveal the supplied Saudi Arabia map as a simplified flat shape on a deep navy background. Keep internal details minimal and remove or hide any tiny unreadable labels. Use a soft camera zoom toward the western region and place one aqua location pin to represent Jeddah. Do not draw exact streets or claim coverage outside approved brand information. A small service-origin dot appears away from the destination. Animate a glowing aqua route from the origin dot to the Jeddah pin. Two or three dots travel along the route. When the route reaches the pin, the pin produces one clean circular pulse. Then move the car icon a short distance toward the pin with a restrained cyan speed trail.

MOTION:
- Map reveal: mask/wipe from bottom to top
- Camera zoom: smooth 8% toward the western region
- Route: path draw followed by moving dots
- Pin: scale 85% to 100%, then one ripple pulse
- Car: short path-follow movement, no deformation

PALETTE:
Deep navy #071A2B, aqua #31D6D0, cyan #7DEFF0, white #FFFFFF, silver #C8D2D9, tiny gold accent #E7B85A.

COMPOSITION:
Map centered in the middle 60% of the frame. Keep the upper area clean for CapCut typography. Keep the final destination visually obvious without adding extra pins or clutter.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No generated text, no street names, no fake city labels, no invented coverage zones, no logo, no watermark, no realistic map satellite imagery, no extra route network, no distorted map, no deformed car, no flicker, no glitch, no people.
