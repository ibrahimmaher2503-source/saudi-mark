Flat vector motion, soft edges, premium easing, no text, no logo, no watermark, no photorealistic water, no splash chaos.

## Transition C — Pin to Phone

Create a clean 8-frame premium transition where a cyan booking-confirmation ring expands outward, becomes a thin aqua light sweep, and reveals a polished car silhouette in a dark navy end-card environment. Smooth commercial motion design, no text, no logo, no watermark, no realistic people.
