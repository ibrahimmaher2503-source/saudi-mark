# Omni Prompt 02 — Car Needs Care

Create a premium fully animated vertical motion-graphics clip for Saudi Mark, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

FORMAT:
- Vertical 9:16
- 3 seconds
- 2D vector animation with light 2.5D depth
- Mostly white background with navy, silver, and aqua accents
- No live action, no realistic people, no photography
- No readable text, no Arabic or English letters, no logo, no watermark
- Leave the upper third clear for Arabic copy in CapCut

USE AS VISUAL REFERENCES:
- supplied car.svg as the exact car silhouette
- supplied clock.svg and calendar-event.svg as schedule icons
- supplied droplet.svg as a water symbol

ACTION:
Place the supplied car icon large in the center. Add a small number of soft grey dust particles and tiny abstract dirt marks around the lower body and wheels. The dust must remain graphic and elegant, never photorealistic. Place the clock and calendar icons around the car as floating schedule symbols. Animate the icons in small controlled circular orbits. The dust particles swirl for a moment, then dissolve as an aqua glow begins to trace around the car. Finish with a thin aqua horizontal wipe entering from the right edge and filling the frame for the next transition.

MOTION:
- Slow 2% push-in on the car
- Dust: loose circular movement, then fade out
- Icons: gentle floating motion with 2–4 degree rotation
- Aqua outline: draws around the car from front to back
- Transition wipe: smooth, fast, horizontal, no hard flash

COLOR AND LIGHT:
Use navy for line art, silver-grey for dust, aqua for the cleaning promise, and one tiny gold highlight on the calendar edge. Keep the background bright and uncluttered.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No generated text, no logo, no fake prices, no fake offers, no realistic dirty car, no smoke, no excessive dust, no deformed car, no extra wheels, no people, no random letters, no watermark, no flicker, no chaotic movement.
