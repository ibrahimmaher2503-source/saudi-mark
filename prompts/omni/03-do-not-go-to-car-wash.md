# Omni Prompt 03 — Do Not Drive to the Car Wash

Create a premium fully animated vertical motion-graphics clip for Saudi Mark, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

FORMAT:
- Vertical 9:16
- 3 seconds
- Minimal 2D vector animation with polished commercial motion design
- Deep navy background after the transition
- No live action, no people, no photography
- No readable text, no Arabic or English letters, no logo, no watermark
- Keep a clean central area for a short Arabic message to be added in CapCut

USE AS VISUAL REFERENCES:
- supplied car.svg for the customer car
- supplied building-skyscraper.svg for a distant generic service destination
- supplied route.svg or map-route.svg for the route line

ACTION:
Begin with an aqua water-ripple wipe covering a white background. Resolve into a deep navy field. Place the car icon on the left and a small distant generic building/service symbol on the right. Draw a long curved aqua route line between them. Animate three small dots traveling slowly along the route, intentionally communicating distance and wasted time. After one second, a clean diagonal navy-and-aqua slash shape sweeps across the route and stops the movement. The car remains still. End with a calm aqua glow around the car, preparing the next scene.

CAMERA AND TRANSITION:
- Start with a fast but smooth aqua ripple
- Slight 4% zoom-out after the navy background appears
- Route line draws from left to right
- Slash shape moves diagonally with a crisp ease-out
- No shake and no aggressive glitch effects

COMPOSITION:
Car on left third, destination on right third, route through the center. Leave a clean upper-center area for the CapCut headline.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No generated words, no street labels, no exact business building, no fake logo, no fake location claims, no realistic traffic, no people, no distorted car, no unreadable text, no watermark, no chaotic particles, no glitch, no flicker.
