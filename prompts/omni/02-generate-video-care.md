# Omni Gemini Video Prompt 02 — Car Needs Care

Generate a complete 3-second vertical video clip, not a still image, for Saudi Mark, a premium mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

OUTPUT:
- 9:16 vertical social-media video
- 1080x1920
- 30 fps
- exactly 3 seconds
- clean 2D vector motion graphics with subtle 2.5D depth
- mostly white background
- no live action, no photography, no people

REFERENCE IMAGES:
Use `assets/png/car.png` as the exact car silhouette. Use `assets/png/clock.png`, `assets/png/calendar-event.png`, and `assets/png/droplet.png` as the exact icon references. Preserve their silhouettes and proportions.

STYLE:
Minimal premium Saudi Mark identity: deep navy #071A2B, white #FFFFFF, aqua #31D6D0, cyan #7DEFF0, silver #C8D2D9, tiny gold accent #E7B85A. Graphic dust must be soft grey dots, never photorealistic dirt.

START FRAME:
At 0.0 seconds, the car is centered on a bright white background. The clock and calendar icons are small and positioned around it. The upper third is empty for text overlay.

ANIMATION:
1. From 0.0 to 0.7 seconds, make the clock, calendar, and droplet icons float gently into their positions.
2. From 0.5 to 1.7 seconds, animate 8–12 small grey dust particles moving in loose circular paths around the lower car body and wheels.
3. From 1.2 to 2.2 seconds, fade the dust particles out one by one while an aqua outline draws around the car from front to rear.
4. At 2.1 seconds, animate the droplet icon through a short downward arc and make it create a small aqua ripple near the car.
5. From 2.5 to 3.0 seconds, send a thin aqua horizontal wipe from right to left, filling the frame to prepare the next clip.

CAMERA:
Use a subtle 2% push-in toward the car. Keep the camera locked and stable. Do not rotate the complete composition.

END FRAME:
The final frame is mostly aqua from the wipe, with the car barely visible behind it, ready to cut into a deep-navy scene.

DO NOT GENERATE:
No readable text, no Arabic letters, no English letters, no logo, no watermark, no price, no offer, no realistic dirty car, no smoke, no excessive dust, no people, no distorted car, no extra wheels, no flicker, no random symbols, no chaotic motion.
