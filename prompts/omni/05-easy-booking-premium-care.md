# Omni Prompt 05 — Easy Booking and Premium Care

Create a premium fully animated vertical motion-graphics clip for Saudi Mark, a mobile car-wash service in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

FORMAT:
- Vertical 9:16
- 4 seconds
- Polished 2D vector animation with subtle 2.5D depth
- Deep navy background with aqua and cyan highlights
- No live action, no realistic people, no photography
- No readable text, no Arabic or English letters, no logo, no watermark
- Leave a clean area for Arabic copy in CapCut

USE AS VISUAL REFERENCES:
- supplied device-mobile.svg as the phone silhouette
- supplied calendar-event.svg for booking
- supplied map-pin.svg for location
- supplied droplet.svg for water
- supplied bubble.svg for soap bubbles
- supplied sparkles.svg for controlled sparkle effects

ACTION:
Match-cut from the glowing Jeddah location pin into a large mobile phone entering from the bottom center. The phone rises with a smooth spring-like ease and rotates no more than 4 degrees. Inside the phone, show abstract interface blocks only: a blank rounded booking card, a calendar icon, a location pin, and a simple confirmation circle. Do not generate interface text.

Animate a small abstract cursor or tap circle pressing the booking area once. On the tap, create a soft cyan confirmation pulse and a tiny restrained gold accent. Around the phone, animate one aqua water droplet on a short arc, two or three white soap bubbles rising and scaling down, and two sparkle bursts timed to the booking confirmation. A thin aqua wave crosses the lower third from left to right.

MOTION:
- Phone: rise from bottom, settle softly
- Camera: 3% push-in during the tap
- Tap: one clear pulse, no repeated clicks
- Droplet: short curved path
- Bubbles: slow upward drift
- Sparkles: brief, precise, not excessive

COMPOSITION:
Phone centered in the middle. Keep the upper third available for copy and the bottom corners free of clutter. Maintain high contrast and large simple shapes.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No generated words, no fake app name, no fake price, no fake phone number, no fake logo, no readable UI text, no broken phone, no floating fingers, no realistic hand, no people, no watermark, no excessive bubbles, no chaotic particles, no flicker, no deformation.
