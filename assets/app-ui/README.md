## Product and visual language observed

- Dark navy application background with slate-blue cards.
- Bright Saudi Mark blue for primary actions and selected states.
- White headings with muted blue-gray supporting text.
- Rounded cards, outlined controls, pill-like status blocks, and strong bottom navigation.
- English-first UI labels with Arabic content and mixed bilingual elements.
- Booking flow organized around service, zone, date, slot, vehicle, location, gift, add-ons, summary, and confirmation.
- Product ecosystem includes packages, subscriptions, memberships, rewards/points, free gifts, vehicle profiles, location selection, and service tracking/trust metrics.

## Important content references

The screenshots visibly include the following product concepts and sample values:

- Home: customer rating, completed services, on-time commitment, packages, subscriptions, bookings, profile, and points.
- Booking: “Book for me”, “Gift this service”, “Fancy Polish Package”, Jeddah Central/North/South, date and time slots.
- Service price shown in the reference: 399 SAR for the Fancy Polish Package.
- Free gifts: Saudi Mark mug and Saudi Mark sunshade.
- Vehicle examples: Hyundai Accent, Toyota Corolla, Toyota Yaris, and Mazda sedan.

These values are **UI reference content only**. Do not treat them as approved live pricing, guaranteed metrics, or final production copy without explicit owner confirmation.

## Usage rules

1. Preserve the Saudi Mark visual language when creating new screens or marketing mockups.
2. Treat the screenshots as product references, not as permission to publish unverified prices, ratings, completion counts, or service-level claims.
3. Keep the official logo and mascot references separate from application screenshots.
4. Do not crop out or rewrite brand marks when using these files as design references.
5. Before production release, confirm Arabic/English copy, metrics, prices, zones, and availability with the brand owner.
